FAQs

How does URL shortening work?

URL shortening converts a long web address into a shorter link that redirects visitors to the original destination.

Is it possible to customise my short URL?

Yes. Custom aliases and branded links can make your shortened URLs easier to recognise and share.

Can I track how many people click my links?

Link analytics can help you monitor clicks and understand how people interact with your links.

Are the links and QR codes free?

Scissor offers a free plan. Available features depend on the plan you choose.

What is a branded domain?

A branded domain lets you use a domain associated with your brand in your shortened links.
